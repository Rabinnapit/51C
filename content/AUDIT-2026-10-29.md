# Content and flow audit, 29 October 2026 (version 0.22.0)

A full read of the course material, from the lessons to the practice test, checked against the FAR Overhaul (RFO) text
on acquisition.gov (the deviation-guide pages for Parts 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 17, 18, 22, 32, 33, 37,
42, 43, 44, 46, 47 and 49, read 29 October 2026) and DFARS Part 237, plus a walk through the learn-to-test flow on a phone
and a desktop in light and dark mode and at every text size.

## Corrections made

1. **Lesson 1, Guiding principles.** The lesson still taught the pre-overhaul vision (best value, public trust, public
   policy). It now teaches the seven RFO guiding principles in FAR 1.102(a), the permission principle in 1.102(b)(4)
   and risk management in 1.102(c), and says the old wording is what older slides quote.
2. **Lesson 3, How to read a citation.** RFO 15.404 is "Cost and/or price analysis", not "Proposal analysis".
3. **Lessons 9 and 27, glossary, two questions.** DFARS 237.872 does not exist; service contract surveillance (the QASP) is
   DFARS 237.172.
4. **Lesson 9.** The personal services prohibition is FAR 37.201-1 (37.201-2 lists the characteristics); the brand-name
   justification cite no longer names a paragraph the RFO text may not have.
5. **Lesson 10, lesson 18, glossary, one question.** Under the RFO, FAR 5.103 covers only FFRDC and bundling notices.
   Sources sought notices and RFIs now point to FAR 10.001(c) and 15.101(c).
6. **Lesson 8, glossary, one question.** F.o.b. origin and destination are defined in FAR 47.303 (47.302 is the
   place-of-delivery policy); risk of loss is FAR 46.505.
7. **Lesson 17 example** listed 52.212-5, which the same lesson says the RFO reserved. Now 52.212-1, -2 and -4.
8. **Lesson 22, glossary, two questions.** The competitive range is "the evaluated proposals best suited for further
   negotiation", which may be limited to the most highly rated; clarifications may happen at any time after proposals
   arrive and never allow a revision. The old "most highly rated proposals" wording was removed everywhere.
9. **Lesson 33.** The contingency credential is CCON 015B (it said 015A).
10. **Question Q1605.** Sealed bidding awards to the bidder whose bid is lowest on price and price-related factors ("best
    price" was loose).
11. **Question Q1103.** Cite is FAR 8.103 and 8.104 (the order of sources and existing contracts), not 8.101.
12. **Glossary.** Added PIID, GSAR, CBAR, QAR, SSDD, DFAS and EPA, each of which the lessons or questions use.
13. **Cites verified and kept** although a script flagged them as "old" numbers: FAR 15.403-4 (now the certificate of
    current cost or pricing data), 15.404-1(b) (price analysis techniques), 33.103 (protests, general) and 33.104 (agency
    protests). All exist under the RFO with the meaning the guide gives them.

## Flow improvements

- **Check yourself is short again.** Some lessons had 26 to 32 questions mapped to them once the B2B class and
  test-taker questions were added, so the check at the end of lesson 18 ran to 32 questions. It now shows up to six of
  the lesson's own questions and offers "Practice all N questions on this lesson" for the rest.
- **Keyboard in the Practice test.** 1 to 4 (or A to D) answer, Enter or → goes to the next question; in the exam
  simulator ← goes back and F flags. Keys stay quiet while typing. The shortcut list in Settings & help → Keyboard grew.
