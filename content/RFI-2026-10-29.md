**Subject:** RFI: certification process details for a free 51C study aid (B2B Exam Prep)

Sir/Ma'am,

I am SFC Rabin Napit, a 51C with the 411th Contracting Support Brigade. On my own time I built B2B Exam Prep, a free
study aid that walks new 51Cs and 1102s from in-processing to the CON 3990V exam (rabinnapit.github.io/51C). It is
not an official product and says so. Its lessons and practice questions were written from the public WarU and DPCAP
documents and checked against the FAR Overhaul text, but a few points about the Army process are not published
anywhere I can read them. I would be grateful for answers to any of the questions below, in any format. I will cite your
office as the source in the app, or leave it unnamed if you prefer.

1. **AITAS approval routing.** After the supervisor approves a CON course or exam request in AITAS, who approves it next
   for a Soldier in a contracting support brigade (role or office)? Is the routing different for a CON 3990V exam
   request than for a course?

2. **Certification request in CAMP.** Once the five courses, the exam and 12 months of experience are complete, who
   submits the certification request in CAMP (which role is "authorized"), what documents are attached, and how long
   does approval usually take? Does the Soldier do anything in CAMP personally?

3. **Missing the 36-month deadline.** When a Soldier will not complete the certification inside the 36-month window,
   what is the process: an exception to policy, requested by whom and approved at what level? How long does an approved
   exception cover, and what happens if it is not granted?

4. **Credit for the 51C transition course.** Does the 9-week Army Acquisition Transition Course at TAAS give credit for
   any certification course (CON 1100V, 1200V, 1300V, 7300V, 1400V) or for the FAC/CON prerequisite modules before
   CON 1300V?

5. **The 6-month local goal.** Most brigades expect the courses and the exam inside 6 months. Is that a published CSB or
   ACC policy, and if so, which document and date?

6. **Enhanced postaward debriefings.** Under DoW's RFO class deviation for DFARS Part 215, is the enhanced debriefing rule
   (2 business days for the offeror's follow-up questions, 5 business days for the response) still in force, and what is
   its current DFARS cite?

7. **Automated contract closeout.** Under the RFO deviations, what are the current criteria and cite for automated
   closeout (formerly DFARS PGI 204.804-3: firm-fixed-price up to $500,000)?

8. **Certified cost or pricing data flow-down.** With the DoW prime-contract threshold at $10 million for awards after
   30 June 2026, what threshold applies to subcontracts under the DFARS Part 215 class deviation?

9. **System roles in week one.** Which system roles should a newly arrived 51C request on arrival (ACWS, PIEE with
   WAWF, EDA and JAM, GFEBS, VCE Paperless Contract File, CPARS, SAM.gov), who approves each, and what is the normal
   turnaround? The app currently says two to three weeks.

10. **The exam itself.** Are the pool size (about 600 questions) and the number of unscored trial questions (about 10)
    official figures, and are any changes to CON 3990V planned for FY27 that new Soldiers should know about?

Thank you for your time. Anything you can share will go straight into a tool that helps new Soldiers get certified on
time.

V/R,
