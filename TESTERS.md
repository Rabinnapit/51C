# Testing B2B Guide 0.9 with new 51Cs and 1102s

## Who to ask

Five to ten people in their first year: a mix of new 51Cs (fresh from AATC), new 1102s or interns, and one or two
supervisors or mentors. At least two should try it on a NIPR computer and two on a personal phone.

## A note you can send (copy and edit)

> Subject: Help test B2B Guide, a study and certification guide for new contracting folks
>
> You are one of the first people to try B2B Guide, a plain-language guide to the DoW Contracting Professional
> certification (the five CON courses, the CON 3990V exam and the 12 months of experience).
>
> How to open it: [website link, if github.io opens on your network] or open the attached B2B-Guide.html (if it came as
> Word documents, follow "READ ME FIRST").
>
> Please spend 30 minutes over the next week:
> 1. Answer the three questions on the first screen and look at My plan. Do the dates make sense for you?
> 2. Do one lesson and its "Check yourself" questions. Try the Listen button and tap a dotted-underlined acronym.
> 3. Take a Quick 10 in Practice test, then try 10 flashcards.
> 4. Open Certification 101 and find out how to sign up for the exam.
>
> Then use Help → "Send tester feedback" (it opens an email with five short questions), or just reply to this email.
> This is an unofficial training aid; double-check anything you rely on with your training office.

## What to look for in the feedback

- Did they understand what to do first without help?
- Anything wrong or out of date (course rules, dates, local procedures)?
- Where they got lost (navigation, words they did not know)?
- Did it work on their device and network (NIPR Edge, phone)? Did progress survive a restart?
- What they would add for someone brand new.

Record fixes in `CHANGELOG.md` and bump `version` in `src/data/meta.json` for the next test round.
