## How to read the badges in the app (as of v1.1.0, 18 Sep 2026)
Official answer = from the PAM list or a 411th deck · AI draft, verify = drafted and reviewed, confirm before relying ·
Open, write your own = no answer in the sources · Verify locally = organization-specific, including the Korea items that
depend on 411th or USFK procedure (the badge's tooltip names C&P) · Check the cite = the citation could not be matched to
the RFO text, or its review is not finished. Citations that were updated with the crosswalk carry no badge; the old-to-new
pair is shown with the answer.
